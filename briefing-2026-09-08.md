### Coalition to Discuss Budget Cuts as Public Opposition to Deficit Hardens
The SPD held an evening meeting on Monday with Prime Minister Andrej Babiš (ANO) and Finance Minister Alena Schillerová on proposals to trim non-mandatory state spending, as pressure builds over the proposed CZK 389 billion 2027 deficit. A Median poll found opposition to the deficit is sharpest among 18–24 year-olds (83%) and university graduates (82%); opposition politicians, including STAN chairman Vít Rakušan, called the deficit "shameful and dangerous" given current low unemployment and inflation. Schillerová has said any real cutting will only start after 2026.
