## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Constitutional Amendment Delayed Amid Turek Dispute
A planned amendment to the Czech Constitution (clarifying rules around ministerial appointments, sparked by the Pavel–Turek standoff) has been **postponed**, with the coalition now waiting for the new composition of the Senate before proceeding.

### Government Approves Parental Allowance Increase and Price-Regulation Bill
The government approved a bill that would let the state regulate prices in the future, and separately approved a draft amendment raising the parental allowance for children born from **January 1, 2027**: from CZK 350,000 to **400,000** (twins/multiples: 700,000 → **800,000**). A related measure restores the **tax exemption on leisure-time employee benefits without a cap**.

### ČEZ Nationalization Continues

## 🚴 Cycling

### Pogačar Smashes 30-Year Alpe d'Huez Record, Extends Lead to 7:11
**Tadej Pogačar** bridged a three-minute gap to breakaway leaders **Richard Carapaz** and **Lenny Martinez** on Stage 19 (Gap → Alpe d'Huez) and dropped them in the final kilometers, winning in a time that **broke Marco Pantani's nearly 30-year-old climbing record** on the legendary ascent. The win extends his overall lead to **7:11** over **Remco Evenepoel**, with **Isaac del Toro** third (+9:42) and **Paul Seixas** now just 24 seconds off the final podium spot. **Lenny Martinez** moved up to 5th overall after his day in the break.

### Today: Stage 20, the Queen Stage — Back to Alpe d'Huez
For the first time ever, the Tour finishes on **Alpe d'Huez on two consecutive days**. Today's 171km stage from Le Bourg-d'Oisans is being billed as the true Queen Stage of this year's race — one of the last chances for a GC shake-up before Paris, with Evenepoel needing to find time on Pogačar and Seixas still hunting the final podium spot.
