Air-raid sirens sounded in eastern Poland overnight as Russia launched a massive missile barrage against Ukraine; Polish radar picked up an unidentified object crossing into Polish airspace near **Lublin**, prompting a scramble of **F-16s**. A crater was later found in a field and is being examined by police, firefighters, and the military. ### Clásica San Sebastián Runs Today — Evenepoel Chases History
Five days after the Tour de France wrapped in Paris, the pro peloton regroups today for the 45th **Clásica San Sebastián** (a.k.a. ### Recap: Pogačar Seals Historic Fifth Tour Title
