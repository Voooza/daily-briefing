## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Tripartita Agrees to Raise Minimum Wage to 24,900 Kč
Government, unions, and employers agreed on August 24 to raise the Czech minimum wage from 22,400 to **24,900 Kč** starting January 2027, with a further rise to roughly 26,900 Kč penciled in for 2028 as part of a long-term plan to reach 47% of the average wage by 2029. Unions argue the pace is still too slow, noting Czechia's minimum wage remains among the lowest in the EU — below neighboring Poland's. The proposal now goes to the government for final approval.

### Doctors at Prague's VFN Revive Patient After Hour-Long Cardiac Arrest
Physicians at Prague's General University Hospital (VFN), working with the city's emergency medical service, successfully revived a patient whose heart had stopped for more than an hour, using an ECMO-based protocol developed in-house. Cardiologist Jan Bělohlávek noted such patients would have had virtually no chance of survival 10–15 years ago; VFN's own data shows roughly a third of similar patients now survive.

### Ukrainian Strike Reportedly Hits Russian Refinery in Krasnodar Overnight
