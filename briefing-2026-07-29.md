## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Pavel Vetoes Budget-Law Amendment; Coalition Vows Override
President **Petr Pavel** vetoed an amendment to the public-budget laws on July 22, arguing it would let the government borrow **without a spending limit for ten years (2026–2036)** and let it exceed the approved budget by up to 10% based solely on its own advisory body's decision, bypassing the Chamber of Deputies. PM **Andrej Babiš** (ANO) called the veto "irresponsible" and says the coalition — which holds 108 of 200 seats, comfortably above the 101 needed — will move to override it. The opposition welcomed Pavel's move.

## 🚴 Cycling

### Pogačar Wins Record-Equaling 5th Tour de France
**Tadej Pogačar** sealed his **fifth Tour de France title** in Paris on Sunday, finishing in 73:56:26 — nearly six and a half minutes ahead of runner-up **Remco Evenepoel** — putting him level with Anquetil, Merckx, Hinault, and Induráin as the winningest riders in race history. The final stage itself (started from Thoiry, finishing via Montmartre and the Champs-Élysées) was won by **Mathieu van der Poel** in a dramatic, shortened-stage finale. UAE Team Emirates-XRG took home more than a third of the race's total prize pot.

### Next Up: Commonwealth Games Cycling
Track and road cycling at the **2026 Commonwealth Games** runs **July 30 – August 2** at the Sir Chris Hoy Velodrome in Glasgow — the next event on the calendar now that the Tour has wrapped.
