## 🤖 AI & Technology

### Kimi K3's Open Weights Go Live — Largest Open Model Ever
Moonshot AI released the full weights of **Kimi K3** on July 27 at 00:00 UTC, making the record-setting **2.8-trillion-parameter** Mixture-of-Experts model (104B active per token) free to download from Hugging Face under a modified MIT license. It ships with a 1M-token context window, native multimodal input (text, image, video), an OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible API, and a new "Kimi Delta Attention" mechanism claimed to give up to 6.3x faster decoding. At 1.4TB, self-hosting is only realistic for well-resourced labs, but it's a major escalation in the open-weight race and follows DeepSeek V4's stable release on July 24.

### Anthropic Ships Claude Opus 5
Anthropic rolled out **Claude Opus 5** across all platforms on July 24, pitched as "close to Fable 5-level intelligence at half the price" of frontier competitors. Pricing holds at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens versus Opus 4.8, with a new low/medium/high effort toggle to trade cost for capability. Opus 5 becomes the default model for Claude Max subscribers and leads on benchmarks like Frontier-Bench and GDPval-AA for software engineering and knowledge work.

### EU Forces Google to Open Android to Rival AI Assistants
The European Commission adopted binding Digital Markets Act requirements ordering Google to open Android to competing AI assistants — including Claude and ChatGPT — by July 2027, with voice activation, screen context, and app actions all mandated. Google must also share portions of its search data with competitors.

## 🚴 Cycling

### Tadej Pogačar Wins His Fifth Tour de France
The 2026 Tour de France concluded Sunday, July 26, in Paris with **Tadej Pogačar** claiming his **fifth title**, joining Eddy Merckx, Miguel Indurain, Jacques Anquetil, and Bernard Hinault as the only riders with five wins. He finished **6:26** clear of **Remco Evenepoel**, with 22-year-old **Isaac del Toro** completing the podium in third. The race was disrupted by wildfires in southwest France, forcing route changes including a shortened final stage. **Mathieu van der Poel** capped the race with a sprint win on a shortened Champs-Élysées circuit, edging Jasper Philipsen, Mads Pedersen, Max Kanter, and Olav Kooij.
