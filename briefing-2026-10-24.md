Sources: [Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/racing/tour-de-france-2027-route-revealed), [Cyclingnews](https://www.cyclingnews.com/pro-cycling/racing/tour-de-france-2027-route-unveiled-most-time-trial-kilometres-since-2012-brutal-pyrenean-queen-stage-as-tadej-pogacar-eyes-record-sixth-title/), [Domestique Cycling stage guide](https://www.domestiquecycling.com/en/features/tour-de-france-2027-route-stage-by-stage-guide/).
- **Britain:** three stages open the race (Edinburgh–Carlisle, Keswick–Liverpool, Welshpool–Cardiff by most sources).
- **Time trials:** 71 km total, the most since 2012. Stage 7 Blois–Chambord (38 km) and stage 16 in Toulouse (33 km).
- **Summit finishes:** stage 9 Grand Colombier, stage 10 Sallanches, stage 18 Col du Portet. Stage 18 is the queen stage, with about 5,400 m of climbing over the Soulor, Tourmalet, Hourquette d'Ancizan and Portet.
- **Other mountain stages:** stage 11 Les Saisies, stage 14 Mende, stage 19 Laruns.
- **Finale:** the last stage climbs Montmartre before the Champs-Élysées.
- Cyclingnews frames it as Tadej Pogačar chasing a record sixth title.
- **Source discrepancies:** outlets disagree on the type of some stages (12, 13, 21) and on the Stage 3 start. Check the official ASO route.
- Women's race: July 30 – August 7, starting in Leeds.
