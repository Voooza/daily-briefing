## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Perseid Meteor Shower Peaks Tonight Under a Moonless Sky
The **Perseid meteor shower** reaches its main maximum overnight between August 12 and 13, roughly 02:00–04:00 UTC (around 4–6 a.m. Czech time), with up to 150 meteors an hour visible at peak. This year's timing is unusually favorable: the new moon falls on the same night, leaving skies maximally dark — astronomers describe the alignment as one that comes around only a few times a decade, with the next equally good pairing not due until 2045. The shower caps a busy skywatching week that began with Wednesday evening's partial solar eclipse over Czechia. Separately, the political standoff between President **Petr Pavel** and PM **Andrej Babiš's** government continues to simmer after Pavel's veto of changes to budget rules last month — the first veto of Babiš's term — with Babiš saying the decision "greatly distressed" him.

### Other Headlines
- Perseid activity remains visible on a lower level through August 24, so early risers still have a shot at meteors in the coming days even past peak night.
- Continued hot, dry weather has prompted precautionary closures at some Prague outdoor venues.

### Czech Tour and Arctic Race of Norway Both Roll Out Today
Two stage races start simultaneously today, August 13.
