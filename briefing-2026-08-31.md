### Anthropic Warns Infostealer Malware Is Hijacking Claude Accounts
Anthropic is signing out affected users and wiping saved payment methods after identifying that infostealer malware — Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine and Acreed on Windows, plus Atomic Stealer on a handful of Macs — stole active Claude login sessions and drained usage limits without owners' knowledge. The company is also refunding charges it can identify as unauthorized, though it cautions that signing users out doesn't remove the malware itself, so a fresh login can be stolen the same way.
