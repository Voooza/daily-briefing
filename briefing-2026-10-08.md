## 💻 Technology

### Microsoft and Nvidia Launch Surface Laptop Ultra (RTX Spark)
At yesterday's San Francisco event, Microsoft launched the **Surface Laptop Ultra** powered by Nvidia's **RTX Spark** chip:
- **Price:** from **$2,599**. The **Surface RTX Spark Dev Box** starts at **$5,999**.
- **Availability:** preorders are open, shipping **October 16**. Asus, Dell, Lenovo, HP and MSI systems ship the same day.
- **Specs:** 15-inch 4K touchscreen up to 120 Hz, up to **128 GB unified memory**, battery rated up to 15 hours of local video.
- **Claims:** Microsoft says local AI prompts run about 2x faster than a comparable MacBook Pro M5, and video generation about 6x faster. These are vendor claims. Early hands-on reports mention rough drivers on pre-release units, so wait for retail reviews.
- Major Windows 11 changes for agentic AI were announced alongside.

(sources: [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/laptops/microsoft-and-nvidia-launch-surface-laptop-ultra-with-rtx-spark-rtx-spark-preorders-live-now-coinciding-with-major-windows-11-changes-for-agentic-ai), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Microsoft-Surface-Laptop-Ultra-debuts-with-RTX-Spark-128-GB-unified-memory-and-2-599-starting-price.1418420.0.html), [Engadget hands-on](https://www.engadget.com/2280417/surface-laptop-ultra-hands-on-a-beautiful-beast-powered-by-nvidias-rtx-spark/), [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/hardware/surface/surface-laptop-ultra-preorders-live))

## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Elections Start Tomorrow
Municipal and one-third Senate elections run **Friday, October 9 (14:00–22:00) and Saturday, October 10 (08:00–14:00)**. A second Senate round, if needed, is **October 16–17**. Voters choose senators in 27 districts, and about **190,125 candidates** are running for municipal seats.

- **Babiš and the Senate:** Deník N reports the ill prime minister campaigned from his bed in the Česká Lípa region and wants to win a key Senate district. Blesk says he has reversed his earlier plan to abolish the Senate and now wants to control it, with ANO targeting showcase districts such as Vyškov and Česká Lípa.
- **Context:** this is the first popularity test for the ANO–SPD–Motoristé coalition. ANO won only one of 27 seats in 2020.

(sources: [Deník N](https://denikn.cz/2193390/komunalni-volby-2026-sledujte-scitani-hlasu-ve-vasi-obci/), [Blesk](https://volby.blesk.cz/clanek/volby-volby-do-senatu/849804/babis-chtel-senat-zrusit-ted-ho-chce-ovladnout-sazi-na-sestricku-zenu-bez-nohou-i-pravnicke-eso.html), [Český rozhlas](https://cesky.radio.cz/volby-do-senatu-a-komunalni-volby-2026-8898918))

## 🚴 Cycling

### Il Lombardia, Saturday October 10, Without Pogačar
The 120th edition runs **Bergamo → Como, 239 km**, including the Madonna del Ghisallo and a **22 km finishing circuit** with a double ascent of San Fermo della Battaglia. **Tadej Pogačar**, winner of the last five editions, is **not starting**.
