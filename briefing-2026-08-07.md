### Anthropic Wins Injunction Blocking Pentagon's "Supply Chain Risk" Label

## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Elite Rescue Diver Dies Exploring World's Deepest Flooded Cave
**Jan Enčev**, a firefighter with the Kladno air rescue service and an experienced cave diver, died while taking part in an international exploration project at **Hranická propast** (the Hranice Abyss), the world's deepest known flooded cave. He became separated from the dive team during a planned descent and contact was lost; his body was recovered from a depth of 186 meters. Police divers from Frýdek-Místek carried out the recovery, and a criminal investigation for negligent manslaughter has been opened to clarify the circumstances.
