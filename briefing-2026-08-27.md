## 🤖 AI & Technology

### Nvidia Crushes Estimates With $96.2B Quarter, But Stock Wobbles
**Nvidia** posted fiscal Q2 2027 revenue of $96.2 billion (up 106% year-over-year) and EPS of $2.22, both comfortably beating consensus, with data-center revenue alone hitting $89 billion. CEO Jensen Huang guided to roughly 70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028 — a figure he called "supply-constrained" given demand — and issued Q3 guidance of $108 billion. Despite the beat, shares initially slid in after-hours trading on margin concerns before paring losses, a sign investors are growing pickier about the AI trade even as the headline numbers keep climbing.

### OpenAI's In-House "Jalapeño" Chip Reportedly Beats Nvidia's Blackwell
OpenAI showcased benchmark results for **Jalapeño**, its first custom inference chip built with Broadcom, claiming 1.5–1.9x more AI work per watt than Nvidia's Blackwell systems and 1.7–4.1x lower latency on interactive workloads. Independent analysis from SemiAnalysis found it also edges out Nvidia's newer Vera Rubin platform on tokens-per-megawatt — notable timing just as Nvidia reported earnings. The chip is inference-only and expected to go live in OpenAI's infrastructure by year-end.

### Also Notable
- **Meta** agreed to pay up to **$16.68 billion** to settle a 29-state lawsuit alleging it designed Facebook and Instagram to be addictive to teens; the deal mandates daily time caps, overnight restrictions, and stronger age verification for young users.
- **Amazon** is shutting down **Mechanical Turk** on September 30 after 21 years, closing the crowdsourced human-labor marketplace Jeff Bezos once called "artificial artificial intelligence" — a casualty of AI now doing the work it used to help train.
- **Samsung** holds a Galaxy Event today expected to unveil the **Galaxy S26 FE**, bringing flagship camera and Galaxy AI features to a cheaper device, alongside a new LPDDR5X-PIM memory design that moves AI processing logic next to DRAM cells.

## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Parliament Overrides Pavel's Veto on Budget Rules
The Chamber of Deputies voted to override President Petr Pavel's veto of amended budget-responsibility legislation, as the governing coalition had a comfortable majority to push the changes through.

### Czech Railways CEO Resigns
**Michal Krapinec** stepped down as CEO of **České dráhy** (Czech Railways); he will be replaced by **Lenka Hamplová**.

### Coalition Proposes Automatic Expulsion for Foreign Offenders
The governing coalition proposed automatically expelling foreigners convicted of crimes carrying at least a three-year sentence, part of a broader package that also includes new stadium rules criminalizing pyrotechnics and face-covering with facial-recognition-based fan bans.
