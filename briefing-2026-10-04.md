## 🤖 AI

### Apple Is Rewriting Full Disk Access Because of Agents
The most consequential AI story of the window is a permissions change. Apple confirmed it will add **"additional controls" to macOS Full Disk Access**, saying some developers use the entitlement in ways that expose "files, mail, messages, and even browsing history" without users understanding the scope. Apple's stated reason is explicit: AI agents have "become increasingly capable and autonomous," and "the risks associated with this level of access will grow substantially."

The trigger context matters more than the announcement. This lands weeks after reports that **Meta's Muse agent was reading messages it wasn't authorised to access**, and after a Wired report on a flaw in ChatGPT's Mac app that could have exposed sensitive data. No ship date yet.

Why this is bigger than it reads: Full Disk Access is a single binary grant designed for backup tools and disk utilities — a human clicks once, a trusted program reads everything. That model assumed the grantee was a program with fixed behaviour. An agent is a program whose behaviour is determined at runtime by a prompt, which may come from a web page it just read. Apple is the first platform owner to say out loud that the old grant is the wrong shape. Expect Microsoft and the Linux desktop stacks to follow, and expect agent vendors to spend Q4 re-architecting around narrower, revocable scopes.

### Musk Confirms TSMC Talks for Terafab
Musk confirmed early Saturday that **Terafab and TSMC are in discussions**, with characteristic hedging — "Just discussions, but something may come of it." TSMC has said nothing.

The shape being floated: TSMC owns and operates the facility, with SpaceX/Terafab either taking an equity stake, committing to guaranteed volume, or both. An alternative has SpaceX holding majority control with TSMC contributing process technology and operational expertise for a smaller stake. Initial investment is reported at **$16.8bn**, for a Texas plant that would **exclusively supply Tesla, SpaceX and xAI** — AI, robotics and space silicon. **Intel joined Terafab as a partner in April 2026** and remains the only other named manufacturing partner.

Read it alongside the Broadcom–Anthropic financing from Friday and a pattern is obvious: every frontier AI operation is now trying to convert a supplier relationship into something structurally closer to ownership. Musk is doing it with fabs, Anthropic with leased TPUs and convertible notes, Amazon with off-balance-sheet chip vehicles. Nobody wants to be a customer in this market.

### Amazon Puts $1bn Into Data Centre Communities, Drops NDAs
Amazon announced **$1 billion over five years** into communities hosting its data centres — education, job training, water preservation, local priorities — and separately said it **no longer uses NDAs** in data centre siting negotiations. The NDA disclosure is the more revealing of the two: non-disclosure agreements with local officials during site selection have been one of the loudest specific grievances in the opposition campaigns.

The numbers behind the concession: an Annenberg survey in August found **61% of US adults oppose data centre construction in their area — up 12 points in four months**. July alone saw **142 protests across 42 states**. **Twenty states have enacted moratoriums or bans**, with seven more advancing restrictions. CNBC's framing is that America's fight is a preview for Europe, Asia and Africa.

This is now electoral. Data centres are a defined flashpoint in the 2026 midterm races, with candidates in both parties being forced to take positions on electricity costs, water use and local control. A $1bn community fund against that is a rounding error on capex — but dropping the NDAs is a real change in how these deals get done.
(sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/03/data-center-backlash-europe-asia-africa.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/03/amazon-responds-to-data-center-backlash-says-it-no-longer-uses-ndas/), [WTOP](https://wtop.com/national/2026/10/amazon-to-invest-1b-into-data-center-communities-amid-backlash-against-data-center-rollouts/))

## 🇨🇿 Czech Republic

### Pavel Will Miss the Municipal Vote — Over a Diary Mistake
President **Petr Pavel** will not vote in next weekend's municipal elections. He left Czechia on **October 2** on a private trip — reported as the **Morocco Rally** — and his public schedule does not resume until **October 19**. Municipal and first-round Senate voting is **October 9–10**.

Prague Castle's framing is a flat admission: a **planning error**, which the President regrets. The practical effect is narrow — he misses only the municipal ballot in **Černouček**, the north Bohemian village where he holds permanent residence — and the Castle says he remains in continuous contact and available if anything requires him. But the optics are not great for a president who has spent the year in open friction with the Babiš coalition, and the opposition will not let it pass quietly.
(sources: [Radio Prague International](https://english.radio.cz/president-pavel-miss-municipal-elections-due-private-trip-morocco-8898971), [expats.cz](https://www.expats.cz/czech-news/article/pavel-to-miss-czech-local-elections-after-private-trip-planning-error), [Around Prague](https://aroundprague.cz/en/news/politics/czech-president-pavel-to-skip-municipal-elections-for-private-trip-abroad))

### The Elections Themselves: First Real Test of the Coalition
**October 9–10** is the first national count since the ANO–SPD–Motorists cabinet took office on **15 December 2025**. **27 of 81 Senate seats** are up; run-offs where needed land **October 16–17**. ANO holds eight of sixteen ministries, Motorists four, SPD three.

The useful frame is that the coalition has governed more cautiously than it campaigned — no rupture with Brussels or Kyiv, but a steady rhetorical line and real changes at the margin, including Babiš saying Czechia will stop contributing to the Prague-led ammunition initiative for Ukraine. The government **survived a no-confidence vote on October 1**, brought over the widening budget deficit and conflict-of-interest concerns. Municipal elections are a poor read on national sentiment in any country, and especially here where local lists dominate — but the Senate third is a real signal, and the opposition has historically over-performed in Senate races.

## 🥊 MMA

### UFC 332: A Record Card, and a New Flyweight Champion
**UFC 332** ran Saturday night at the **Delta Center in Salt Lake City**, and the numbers are genuinely historic. The card produced **16 knockdowns — a modern-era UFC event record**, **11 knockouts** (promotional record for a single event), and **nine of them in the first round** (also a record).

**Natália Silva def. Wang Cong, UD (48-47, 48-47, 49-46)** for the **vacant women's flyweight title**. Silva built an early lead, scored a **second-round head-kick knockdown** — the 16th knockdown of the night and the one that set the record — then managed a tight back half. Two of three judges had it within a round, so the title changed hands on a genuinely close fight rather than a coronation.

**Payton Talbott def. Deiveson Figueiredo, TKO at 2:09 of R1.** This was the performance of the night and the one with consequences. Talbott dismantled the two-time former flyweight champion in two minutes and is now **6-1 in the UFC with back-to-back finishes of former champions** — Henry Cejudo, then Figueiredo. He called out **Sean O'Malley** immediately afterwards, which is the fight to make: O'Malley is himself on the cusp of a title shot after Aiemann Zahabi and Song Yadong. Winner gets the belt shot; that is a straightforward, obviously marketable eliminator.

**Also on the card:** **Johnny Walker** knocked out **Mick Parkin** in the first round of his **heavyweight debut** — a genuinely interesting division move for a fighter whose chin has been the limiting factor at 205. **Roman Kopylov** stopped **Ateba Gautier** in the first, ending Gautier's run. **Esteban Ribovics** stopped **King Green** late in the opening round, ending Green's winning streak.
