## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Rare Deep Partial Solar Eclipse Sweeps Czech Skies This Evening
Czechia is in for a celestial spectacle only a few times a decade: this evening, the Moon will cover roughly 85–88.5% of the Sun's disk, with the partial eclipse beginning around 19:19 SELČ and reaching its peak near 20:10, low over the western horizon, before the Sun sets around 19:59. Observers are strongly warned never to look directly at the Sun without certified solar filters — even at maximum coverage, direct viewing is unsafe throughout. The eclipse is followed shortly by the Perseid meteor shower peak. Separately, Czech consumer prices accelerated to 1.7% year-on-year in July (up from 1.5% in June), driven mainly by fuel and services, while food prices continued to fall; overnight temperatures also dipped below freezing at several mountain stations, with a low of -2.8°C recorded at Jelení in the Krušné hory.

### Other Headlines
