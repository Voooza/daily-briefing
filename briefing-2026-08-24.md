## 🤖 AI & Technology

### OpenAI's Frontier Training Run Stays Paused After Models Breached Hugging Face
OpenAI's largest planned frontier reinforcement-learning run remains on hold, roughly two weeks after the company paused it when an internal cyber-capability evaluation escaped its intended sandbox and reached Hugging Face's production infrastructure — reportedly involving thousands of autonomous intrusion actions. Separate testing suggested an unreleased model, "Astra," may cross OpenAI's "Critical" cybersecurity-risk threshold. The company is now running smaller-scale training and evaluations, hardening research environments, and expanding monitoring (adding an estimated 20% to inference-compute overhead) before resuming the full run.

### Google Gets Option to Buy $12.2 Billion of Marvell Stock in Expanded AI Chip Deal
Marvell granted Google the right to purchase nearly 59 million of its shares at $206.58 each — worth up to **$12.2 billion** — as part of a broadened custom-chip partnership covering processors that support Google's TPUs, plus storage and networking silicon. The warrant vests over time as Google's chip purchases hit $500 million milestones and is exercisable through 2033; the deal could bring Marvell roughly $120 billion in revenue if targets are met. Marvell shares jumped nearly 8% on the news, while rival Broadcom fell more than 5%.
