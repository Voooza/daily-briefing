### Anthropic: Expanded Cyber Verification Program
Anthropic unveiled an **expanded Cyber Verification Program on October 6**. It folds the earlier **Project Glasswing** initiative into a **three-tier structure**:
- **Defense Access** for incident response and vulnerability validation
- **Red Team Access** for authorized pen-testing organizations
- **Specialized Access** for safety-critical systems such as flight operating systems and power grids

Separately, Anthropic released **Sonnet 5.5**, pitched as about **30% faster at 30% lower cost** for the same work.
(sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-anthropic-and-openai-unveil.html), [Micro Center, This Week in AI](https://www.microcenter.com/site/mc-news/article/this-week-in-ai-oct-2-2026.aspx))

## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Elections Start Friday
Municipal and one-third Senate elections run **Friday, October 9 (14:00–22:00) and Saturday, October 10 (08:00–14:00)**. A second Senate round, if needed, is **October 16–17**. Only **154 candidates** are running for the Senate, the second-smallest field in its history after 1998. This is the first real test of the ANO–SPD–Motoristé coalition, and ANO wants to do well at city halls in Prague, Brno and Ostrava.
(sources: [Deník](https://www.denik.cz/komunalni-volby/cesko-volebni-kalendar-kdy-ajake-volby-budou/), [České noviny election page](https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/volby-cr/index.php?g=3), [ČT24](https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/tema/senatni-volby-2026-105560))
