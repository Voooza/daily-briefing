## 🚴 Cycling

### Pogačar Abandons Vuelta After Heavy Crash; Enric Mas Inherits the Red Jersey
Race leader Tadej Pogačar crashed hard 31km from the finish of stage 8 and abandoned the Vuelta a España with shoulder and facial injuries, ending his bid for the title. Spaniard Enric Mas inherits the overall lead — the first Spanish rider to wear La Roja since 2018 — 1:11 ahead of Felix Gall, with four-time champion Primož Roglič third at 1:33. The stage itself was won by Bryan Coquard in a crash-marred sprint finish, edging Mads Pedersen on the line in Xeraco.

### Stage 9 Preview: Summit Finish at Alto de Aitana
Today's 187.4km stage from La Vila Joiosa to Alto de Aitana packs six categorized climbs and 5,166m of elevation gain into a mountain stage that reopens the GC battle in Pogačar's absence, finishing atop the 5.8%-average Alto de Aitana.
