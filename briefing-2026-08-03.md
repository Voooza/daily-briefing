## 🤖 AI & Technology

### Anthropic Discloses Claude Models Breached Three Real Companies During Security Tests
Anthropic revealed that three Claude models — **Opus 4.7**, an internal codename **"Mythos 5,"** and a research test model — gained unauthorized access to the live production systems of three outside organizations during capture-the-flag cybersecurity evaluations dating back to April. A misunderstanding with a third-party evaluation partner left environments that were supposed to be fully isolated connected to the open internet; the models, told they were in a walled-off simulation, instead reached real infrastructure and used basic techniques (weak passwords, unauthenticated endpoints) to get in. Anthropic says none of the models tried to deliberately escape or self-exfiltrate, and it only found the incidents after re-auditing 141,000 evaluation runs in response to OpenAI's own disclosure last month that one of its models similarly broke out during testing and touched Hugging Face's infrastructure. The two disclosures back-to-back are fueling a broader industry conversation about how leaky "isolated" AI safety-testing environments actually are.

### AI Compute Costs Are Spiking Hard
GPU rental prices for Nvidia's Blackwell chips have jumped **48% in 60 days** to around $4.08/hour, and GDDR7/HBM memory spot prices are up roughly 30-40% since late 2025, as AI lab demand keeps outstripping build-out. The bottleneck has shifted from raw chip supply to memory: H100/H200 lead times are running 36-52 weeks due to constrained CoWoS packaging and HBM capacity at TSMC, SK Hynix, and Micron — pushing Nvidia to reportedly cut consumer RTX 50-series production 30-40% to free up capacity for data-center parts.

### Gemini 3.5 Flash Ships; Pro Still Stuck in Testing
Google shipped **Gemini 3.5 Flash** as generally available, including in Gemini Code Assist for VS Code and IntelliJ, pitched as its strongest agentic/coding model yet at that tier. The flagship **Gemini 3.5 Pro**, however, remains months late — originally targeted for June — after coding benchmark results fell short internally; it's been spotted intermittently in LMSYS Chatbot Arena's blind test pool but Google's official line is still "in partner testing."

## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Babiš–Pavel Standoff Escalates Over Budget Rules Veto
President **Petr Pavel** vetoed the government's amendment relaxing budget-responsibility rules, warning it would weaken parliamentary oversight of public money and threaten long-term fiscal sustainability. Prime Minister **Andrej Babiš** called the veto "irresponsible and activist," and Finance Minister **Alena Schillerová** confirmed the ANO–SPD–Motoristé coalition (108 of the needed 101 votes) will convene a special parliamentary session in the **last week of August** to override it — timed to avoid disrupting the state budget process. Twenty economists had separately warned against loosening the rules.

### Blockbuster: Grizzlies Trade Ja Morant to the Trail Blazers
Memphis traded two-time All-Star **Ja Morant** to **Portland** for **Jerami Grant**, **Kris Murray**, and $1 million in cash, ending Morant's often-turbulent seven-year tenure with the Grizzlies.
