## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Government Unveils 2027 Budget With Second-Highest Deficit on Record
Finance Minister Alena Schillerová presented a draft 2027 state budget with a deficit of 389 billion Kč — the second-largest in Czech history — with revenues projected to rise 3.3% to 2.189 trillion Kč against spending up 6.2% to 2.578 trillion Kč. The government frames the jump as temporary, driven by healthcare costs and expensive energy; the opposition calls it irresponsible given the current economic growth. The cabinet can still adjust the numbers before sending a final version to the Chamber of Deputies by the end of September.

### Appellate Court Clears SPD Over "Surgeons From Abroad" Campaign Posters
The Prague Municipal Court overturned a lower court's three-million-crown fine against the SPD movement, ruling its controversial 2024 election posters — one invoking a knife-wielding, dark-skinned man over migration policy, another linking Roma children to smoking and truancy — did not amount to incitement to hatred. The panel called the imagery "striking, simplifying and at times provocative, on the edge of good taste" but within the bounds of ordinary campaign rhetoric. A separate case against SPD leader and Chamber of Deputies speaker Tomio Okamura remains on hold after lawmakers declined to waive his immunity.
