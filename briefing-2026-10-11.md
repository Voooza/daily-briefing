## 🚴 Cycling
**Il Lombardia (Bergamo → Como, 239 km), Saturday:** **Paul Seixas** (Decathlon CMA CGM) won solo, becoming the **youngest winner in the race's 120 editions at 20**.
- He attacked on the Civiglio and rode away from the favourites about 19 km out. Winning time 5:49:28.
- **Podium:** 2. Enric Mas (+0:48), 3. Giulio Ciccone (+1:05). Remco Evenepoel was 4th (+1:20).
- It is Decathlon CMA CGM's first Monument win. Tadej Pogačar, winner every year since 2021, was absent.

Sources: [Rouleur](https://www.rouleur.cc/racing/seixas-wins-il-lombardia-2026), [PezCycling News](https://pezcyclingnews.com/racing/il-lombardia-2026-paul-seixas-wins/), [Cyclingnews](https://www.cyclingnews.com/pro-cycling/live/il-lombardia-2026-live-remco-evenepoel-isaac-del-toro-and-paul-seixas-all-in-contention-for-wide-open-final-monument-of-the-year/).
