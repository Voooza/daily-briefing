## 🤖 AI & Technology

### Google DeepMind Overhauls Leadership as Hassabis Steps Back, Jeff Dean Departs After 27 Years
**Demis Hassabis** is stepping back from day-to-day leadership of Google DeepMind to become Alphabet's chief scientist, while **Koray Kavukcuoglu** — DeepMind's CTO and Google's chief AI architect — takes over as senior vice president, reporting directly to CEO **Sundar Pichai**, and will now oversee Gemini model development, frontier research, and the Gemini app teams. In a parallel shake-up, longtime Google chief scientist **Jeff Dean** is leaving after 27 years to launch an AI startup called **Discovery Loop**, focused on models that can improve themselves with little human feedback; he's taking Google Senior Fellow **Sanjay Ghemawat**, DeepMind VP **Oriol Vinyals**, and Google Brain co-founder **Quoc Le** with him.

### Also Notable
- **Meta** was ordered by a New Mexico court to pay an additional **$567 million** into a child-harms remediation fund — on top of $375M levied in March — after being branded a "public nuisance"; Meta must now hide like-counts from minors, pause push notifications overnight, and cap under-18 usage at ~90 hours/month.
- **Tesla and SpaceX** are jointly building a $16.8 billion chip-manufacturing complex ("Terafab") in Grimes County, Texas, to produce memory and logic chips for Tesla vehicles, Optimus robots, and SpaceX computing systems.
- The **EU AI Act**'s high-risk-system requirements entered full force on August 2, covering AI used in employment, education, critical infrastructure, financial services, and law enforcement.
- New model releases continue to stack up, including **DeepSeek-V4-Flash** and **GPT-5.6 "Luna"** from OpenAI, as the pace of frontier launches keeps accelerating.

## 🚴 Cycling

### Niewiadoma Seizes Yellow with Stunning Solo Raid on Mont Ventoux
**Kasia Niewiadoma-Phinney** produced the ride of the Tour de France Femmes so far, launching a long-range attack nine kilometers from the summit of **Mont Ventoux** and soloing to the finish 1:16 ahead of **Demi Vollering**, with **Elisa Longo Borghini** third at 1:42. It's Niewiadoma-Phinney's first-ever Tour stage win despite having won the general classification before, and it vaults her into yellow, 15 seconds ahead of Vollering with **Marlen Reusser** third overall. The race now heads toward Sunday's finish in Nice via today's flat, sprint-friendly **Stage 8** from Sisteron.

### Tour de Pologne Heads to Its Time-Trial Finale
**Bart Lemmen** holds the overall lead into the final stage of the Tour de Pologne, a short individual time trial at the Wieliczka Salt Mine on Sunday, August 9, that will decide the general classification.
