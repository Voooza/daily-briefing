## 🤖 AI

### AI Safety Goes to the New York City Council
Executives from **Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Meta** testified at a **New York City Council committee hearing** on the risks and regulation of AI on October 5. Former Google DeepMind researcher **Alex Turner** warned that "misaligned" AI could prove more dangerous than China's aggressive development push, saying "we are racing to build and grow our own adversary."

A city council taking up frontier-model risk is a signal in itself. It follows last week's Anthropic IPO-prospectus leak and OpenAI's cancellation of GPT-6.1 Astra, and it comes while Washington's new "Super Intelligence Force" is still finding its feet. Regulation pressure is arriving from every level at once.
(sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/05/anthropic-openai-google-meta-execs-testify-nyc-council-ai-hearing.html), [AI Weekly, Oct 5](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-05))

### OpenAI: Ads in Image Generation, 1.2 Billion Weekly Users
OpenAI said Monday it will **test visual ads during ChatGPT image generation** later this month in the US with an initial advertiser group. Ads will be clearly labelled and kept separate from the generated image. The company also said **ChatGPT now reaches 1.2 billion weekly users**.

The scale is what makes ads inevitable. At 1.2 billion weekly users, the question is how much ad load the product will tolerate, not whether it appears.
(sources: [AI Weekly, Oct 5](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-05))

### The US–China Model Gap Is the Smallest Yet Tracked
**DeepSeek V4.1 Flash** scored **81.1 on LiveBench** against **Anthropic's top model at 83.4**, which Bloomberg Intelligence calls the smallest US–China top-model gap it has tracked. The spread is **roughly 3%**, down from about **9% in May** and **15% earlier in 2026**.

One benchmark is a narrow lens. The trend line, though, runs in one direction, and it undercuts the argument that export controls alone keep the gap open. A related detail from The Information: **six of about 30 tenants in one Haidian office building resell Anthropic Claude access**, priced **70–90% below** Anthropic's own prices.
(sources: [AI Weekly, Oct 5](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-05), [Trust Insights AI news](https://trustinsights.substack.com/p/so-what-ai-news-you-should-care-about-987))

## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### First Casualties of Babiš's Conflict of Interest: Eight Agrofert Projects
The State Agricultural Intervention Fund (**SZIF**) did not manage to sign **8 Agrofert projects, worth about 68 million CZK**, before Andrej Babiš became Prime Minister, so it had to terminate them. The companies had applied beforehand, but the conflict of interest meant the fund could not administer them.

Context: Babiš was appointed PM on **December 9** but transferred his Agrofert shares to the newly created **RSVP Trust** only on **February 20**, so he was in a factual conflict of interest for more than three months. Expect this to feature in the campaign for the **October 9–10** municipal and Senate elections.
(sources: [iROZHLAS](https://www.irozhlas.cz/zpravy-domov/stret-zajmu-andrej-babis-szif-zemedelsky-fond-agrofertu-ukoncil-8-projektu-68_2610060500_tec))

### Nosková Out in Beijing
**Linda Nosková**, seeded fifth, lost to **Ekaterina Alexandrova 2–6, 2–6** in the round of 16 at the **China Open** in 68 minutes. She failed to convert any of four break points and lost her own serve twice in each set. She will not repeat last year's final run. Elsewhere in Beijing, **Bartůňková** earlier knocked out Sabalenka, and **Siniaková** is chasing the biggest semifinal of her career.
(sources: [České noviny](https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/noskova-podlehla-v-pekingu-alexandrovove-a-neobhaji-lonske-finale/2883445), [Tenisportal](https://www.tenisportal.cz/zpravy/siniakova-zabojuje-o-nejvetsi-semifinale-v-kariere-dokaze-napodobit-krajanku-noskovou-51882/))
