## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Wildfires in France Draw In Czech Firefighters — and Reshape the Tour de France
A second team of Czech firefighters and a **Black Hawk helicopter** landed near Bordeaux on Friday to help battle the wildfires devastating southwestern France, deployed under the EU Civil Protection Mechanism. The fires — which have burned nearly 98,000 hectares in France (a historic record) and forced over 267,000 evacuations across France and Spain combined — also forced Tour de France organizers to shorten today's final Paris stage from 133km to 89km after police normally assigned to the race were redirected to firefighting duty.

### Minister Mrázová Fined for Concealing Family Loan
Regional Development Minister **Zuzana Mrázová** (ANO) was fined by the Most municipal authority for violating conflict-of-interest law after failing to disclose a roughly **CZK 500,000 family loan** used to buy land in Bílina in her 2024 asset declaration. Her ministry says she acknowledges the error, will pay the fine (capped at CZK 50,000), and won't appeal.

### Czech Republic to Arm Montenegro
Following talks between PM **Andrej Babiš** and Montenegrin PM **Milojko Spajić**, the Czech Republic signed a deal to supply Montenegro's armed forces with **€24.2 million** worth of weapons — roughly 6,500 small arms including CZ BREN 3 rifles, pistols, and sniper rifles from Colt CZ Group.

### Other Domestic Headlines
