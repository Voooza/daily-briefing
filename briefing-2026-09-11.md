### Claude Formalizes Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean
Anthropic announced that Claude produced the first complete, computer-verified formalization of Fermat's Last Theorem — a proof mathematicians expected to take years to formalize. Working largely autonomously over 11 days with a new harness built on the Prove2Me platform, Claude wrote roughly 13 million lines of Lean code and proved over 30,300 intermediate theorems (29,500 used in the final proof), consuming about six billion output tokens. It's now the largest Lean proof ever written.
