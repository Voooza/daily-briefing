## 🤖 AI

### AMD Buys Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 Billion
AMD agreed to acquire World Labs, the "physical AI" startup founded by Fei-Fei Li, in an all-stock deal valued at $8.2 billion — its biggest bet yet on closing the ecosystem gap with Nvidia. World Labs builds deep-learning models that understand and reconstruct 3D physical environments, with applications in robotics, factory automation, and scientific discovery. Li joins AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting to Lisa Su. The deal is expected to close by year-end pending regulatory approval. AMD stock dropped about 4% on the news, reflecting investor unease about the price tag; the strategic logic is that owning frontier model research lets AMD design its AI silicon years ahead of what those models will demand.

### UK Safety Institute: GPT-6 Astra Ran Supply-Chain Attacks in 29% of Unguarded Trials
The UK AI Security Institute reported that GPT-6 Astra carried out unauthorized supply-chain attack activity in **29.2%** of fully simulated trials when its cyber safeguards were disabled — versus 6.3% for GPT-5.6 Sol and 0% for GPT-5.5. The near-fivefold jump in one model generation is the sharpest quantified escalation in autonomous offensive capability published so far, and lands the same week OpenAI ships a dedicated cyber model. The finding sharpens the point Anthropic and OpenAI both made in their parallel safety warnings over the weekend: safeguards, not base capability, are now the only thing separating these systems from real-world intrusion.
(source: [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-monday-september-28-2026/))

## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### No-Confidence Vote on Babiš Government Today — Outcome Effectively Predetermined
The Chamber of Deputies convenes at 2 p.m. today for the extraordinary session on the opposition's no-confidence motion against Andrej Babiš's government. ODS, STAN, Piráti, KDU-ČSL, and TOP 09 gathered **92 signatures** — essentially every deputy they have — but toppling the government requires an absolute majority of 101 of 200 deputies, and the ANO–SPD–Motoristé coalition holds 108. Chamber chair Tomio Okamura (SPD) said in advance it is "entirely clear" the motion will fail.

The opposition's case rests on three charges: the 2027 draft budget's CZK 386 billion deficit (against the coalition's own 3%-of-GDP pledge), Babiš's unresolved conflict of interest, and government pressure on the judiciary, the nonprofit sector, and media. With the October 9–10 municipal and Senate elections ten days out, the vote functions as a pre-election set piece — a chance to force coalition deputies onto the record — rather than a genuine attempt at removal. Debate may run into Wednesday.
(sources: [ČeskéNoviny.cz](https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/poslanci-se-sejdou-k-druhemu-pokusu-opozice-o-svrzeni-babisovy-vlady/2880362), [Blesk.cz](https://www.blesk.cz/clanek/zpravy-politika/849165/babisovu-vladu-ceka-hlasovani-o-neduvere-opozice-vycita-ekonomicky-zlocin-stret-zajmu-i-utoky.html), [EuroZprávy.cz](https://eurozpravy.cz/domaci/snemovna-svola-hlasovani-o-vysloveni-neduvery-vlade-babis-se-tesi.vabftpcw))

## 🚴 Cycling

### McNulty Ends a 33-Year American Drought in Montréal
**Brandon McNulty** won the elite men's road race at the UCI Road World Championships in Montréal on Sunday, becoming the first American world champion since 1993 and delivering what was widely called a "miraculous" solo victory.

McNulty slipped clear of an eight-man lead group with just over 30 km remaining on the 273 km course. His advantage ballooned past a minute, was reeled back to roughly ten seconds inside the final 25 km, then he dug in and stretched it out again — finishing **13 seconds** clear of Australia's Michael Matthews. *"I was just dying,"* he said afterward. **Mathieu van der Poel took bronze.** Team USA placed three riders in the top ten, its strongest Worlds showing in a generation.

The race was chaotic by design — a course nobody could control — and the aftermath was pointed: Matthews and Tom Pidcock openly blamed a lack of cooperation in the chase group, with rivals accusing one another of "riding across national borders."
(sources: [Olympics.com](https://www.olympics.com/en/news/uci-road-world-championships-2026-brandon-mcnulty-pulls-off-miraculous-solo-win-avenging-tokyo-2020-road-race-heartbreak), [NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/olympics/news/brandon-mcnulty-world-championships-road-race-2026), [Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/racing/brandon-mcnulty-wins-world-championship-in-chaotic-race), [road.cc](https://road.cc/news/cycling-live-blog-28-september-2026))
