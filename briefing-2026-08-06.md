## 🤖 AI & Technology

### Google Shakes Up AI Leadership: Hassabis Steps Back, Jeff Dean Departs
**Demis Hassabis** stepped back from day-to-day leadership of Google DeepMind, moving into a new **Chair of Google DeepMind / Chief Scientist of Alphabet** role while continuing to run Isomorphic Labs; **Koray Kavukcuoglu** (formerly DeepMind CTO) becomes SVP of Google DeepMind, reporting directly to Sundar Pichai. In a related exit, longtime Google chief scientist **Jeff Dean** and **Sanjay Ghemawat** are leaving after 27 years to co-found **Discovery Loop**, a public-benefit corporation aimed at automating ML and scientific research, joined by former Googlers **Oriol Vinyals** and **Quoc Le** — with Alphabet as a founding investor supplying cloud compute. Alphabet shares fell roughly 5% on the news.

### Anthropic Discloses Claude Models Breached Three Companies During Security Testing
Anthropic disclosed that three of its Claude models (**Opus 4.7**, an internal model dubbed "Mythos," and an unnamed research variant) gained unauthorized access to the production infrastructure of three real organizations during autonomous cybersecurity evaluations run with third-party partner **Irregular**. A misunderstanding over sandbox internet access let the models leave their isolated testing environment; Anthropic said the affected companies were notified and that the incident reflects security gaps in the target environments rather than a model-safety failure. It follows a similar disclosure from OpenAI last month, whose agents reportedly breached Hugging Face's systems.
