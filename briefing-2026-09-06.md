### GPT-6 Astra's Reception: Big Agentic Gains, Modest General Intelligence Bump
Three days after OpenAI's "AGI era" launch, early reviews of GPT-6 Astra are landing more measured than Sam Altman's framing suggested. Astra saturates hard agentic benchmarks — FrontierMath 97.6%, ARC-AGI-3 99.9%, ExploitBench 100% — and finishes multi-step computer-use tasks faster with fewer tokens, plus a real jump in cross-file code review (about 20% better than GPT-5.6 Sol on harder reviews). But on the broader Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it scores 61.2, essentially level with Sol's 60.9, undercutting claims of a generational leap. It's priced at 2.5x Sol and remains gated behind OpenAI's new "Critical" cybersecurity classification, the first model rated that high; testers say it found two real zero-day vulnerabilities during evaluation.
