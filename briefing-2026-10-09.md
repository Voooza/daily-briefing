## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Elections Open Today
Municipal and one-third Senate elections run **today, Friday October 9 (14:00–22:00), and Saturday October 10 (08:00–14:00)**. A second Senate round, if needed, is **October 16–17**.

- **Senate:** 27 seats are up, with 154 candidates, the second-lowest number in history.
- **Municipal:** about 190,000 candidates for roughly 62,000 seats in 6,255 municipalities. Prague, Brno, Plzeň and Ostrava vote in three elections at once.
- **Counting:** begins right after polls close. Municipal counts often run until Sunday.
- **Context:** these are the first elections held in the first full October week, under the constitutional amendment in force since January 1, 2026. They are also the first popularity test for the ANO–SPD–Motoristé coalition.

No results are available yet.
(sources: [ČT24](https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/odstartuji-volby-do-obecnich-zastupitelstev-a-senatu-378382), [iROZHLAS](https://www.irozhlas.cz/volby/senatni-volby-2026), [Ministry of the Interior](https://mv.gov.cz/volby-referenda/volby-do-senatu-a-zastupitelstev-obci-2026-2))

## 🚴 Cycling
**Il Lombardia, Saturday October 10:** the 120th edition runs 239 km from Bergamo to Como with about 4,600 m of climbing. San Fermo della Battaglia is climbed for the first time with 28 km to go, and a finishing circuit in Como repeats 2022. The Madonna del Ghisallo comes with 61 km left.
- **No Pogačar,** who has won every edition since 2021.
- **Favourites:** World Champion Brandon McNulty (UAE), and European Champion Remco Evenepoel (Red Bull–BORA–hansgrohe). Also in the field: Isaac del Toro, Adam Yates, Jhonatan Narváez, Jan Christen, Tom Pidcock, Mas, Seixas and Jorgenson. Previews call the race wide open.
