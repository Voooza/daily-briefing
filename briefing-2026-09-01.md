### OpenAI's ChatGPT Ads Hit $1 Billion Annualized Run Rate in 200 Days
OpenAI says its ChatGPT advertising business has crossed a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate just six months after launch, a milestone the company is holding up as validation of its diversification push ahead of an anticipated IPO. The self-serve ad platform expanded this week to marketers in India, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa, with ads showing to free-tier and Go-plan users across more than 40 countries — though OpenAI still looks likely to fall short of the $2.4B in 2026 ad revenue it had originally projected.

### Bank of England Chief Warns Frontier AI Threatens Financial Stability
In a letter to G20 finance officials written in his capacity as chair of the Financial Stability Board, Bank of England Governor Andrew Bailey warned that frontier AI models could materially increase cyber risk across the global financial system by making attacks faster, cheaper and more scalable — calling it the "most immediate concern" for markets. He also flagged stretched AI-sector asset valuations and circular debt-financed deals between AI companies and hyperscalers as fragilities that could trigger a disorderly correction, and urged firms to harden defenses against simultaneous, system-wide disruptions.

## 🏀 NBA

### Kawhi Leonard Trade Between Clippers and Raptors Still on Hold
The blockbuster deal agreed June 30 — sending Kawhi Leonard to Toronto for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, unprotected 2031 and 2033 first-round picks, a 2027 first-round pick swap and two second-rounders — remains paused pending the NBA's outside-counsel investigation into whether the Clippers circumvented the salary cap via undisclosed endorsement arrangements with Leonard. ESPN reports Toronto could theoretically renegotiate terms, and Los Angeles says it's comfortable keeping the seven-time All-Star if the Raptors walk away, though the league is expected to resolve the probe quietly and let the trade proceed largely as planned.

### Jonathan Kuminga Signs with Timberwolves Over Lakers Offer
Kuminga agreed to a two-year, $12.4 million deal (with a second-year player option) to fill Minnesota's vacant power-forward slot alongside Anthony Edwards, LaMelo Ball, Jaden McDaniels and Rudy Gobert — choosing a shorter-term "prove-it" contract over more lucrative offers from the Lakers, Bulls and Trail Blazers, with Edwards reportedly playing a role in recruiting him. To create the cap room, Minnesota sent Josh Green and cash to Utah for Cody Williams and John Konchar.

### Also Notable
- Fred VanVleet is opting into his $25M player option for 2026-27 after tearing his ACL in an offseason workout and missing all of last season.
