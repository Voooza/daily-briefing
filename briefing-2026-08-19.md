## 🤖 AI & Technology

### OpenAI Launches ChatGPT for Teens
**OpenAI** began a global rollout of **ChatGPT for Teens**, a separate, age-gated version of the chatbot for 13-17 year-olds that auto-enrolls anyone identified as under 18. It ships with safety guardrails active by default (no romantic language, no encouraging emotional dependence, blocked suicide/self-harm and sexual content), a new **Study Mode** that walks teens through problem-solving instead of handing over answers, homework-cheating redirects, and parental controls including Quiet Hours and safety notifications. The rollout is expected to finish within two weeks; OpenAI also announced a teen AI-literacy partnership with CodeAI.

### OpenAI Ships Ultrafast Mode; Google's Gemini Cadence Keeps Accelerating
OpenAI opened a limited API preview of **Ultrafast mode** for GPT-5.6 Sol, built on Cerebras hardware and delivering roughly 750 output tokens/second — up to 14x faster inference. It follows **Google's Gemini 3.7 Flash** release just three weeks after 3.6 Flash (FrontierCode 1.1 up from 34.4% to 43.6%, AutomationBench up from 17% to 30.4%), underscoring how fast the frontier-model release cycle has compressed this year.

### Also Notable
- **Stripe's** ~$7B+ acquisition of AI-model router **OpenRouter** continues to reverberate as a signal of gateway/infrastructure consolidation in the AI stack.
- **xAI's Grok 4.6** (launched Aug 12) is now matching GPT-5.6 Sol Max on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at the same price point, with context expanded to 500K tokens.
- EU AI Act transparency rules are now live continent-wide, requiring AI systems to identify themselves to users; Anthropic is rolling out machine-readable watermarks on Claude output to comply.

## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### Storms Batter the Country; 550+ Fire Department Callouts
Severe storms swept Czechia on Monday afternoon and into Tuesday, with wind gusts over **100 km/h** recorded in Skuteč. Firefighters logged more than **550 technical assistance calls** in a single six-hour window, downed trees briefly halted rail traffic near Prague's Zbraslav district, and thousands were left without power. Separately, health authorities now believe **norovirus** is the likely cause of the outbreak at the Priessnitz spa in Jeseník that has sickened 111 people over the past week.

### Energy and Politics
Czechia switched on its largest battery-storage facility to date, a **120 MWh** project near Vintířov, as the grid leans further into renewables buffering. On the political front, speculation is building over potential successors should President **Petr Pavel** decline a second term, while the government and Pavel remain at odds over the state budget following the cabinet's return from summer recess. In smaller items: one in five parents expect back-to-school supplies to cost over CZK 5,000 this year, and Czechia's library network — while shrinking — remains the densest per capita in Europe.

## 🏀 NBA

### LeBron James Signs with the 76ers
After the longest star free agency of the century, **LeBron James** has agreed to a two-year, $8 million deal with the **Philadelphia 76ers**, choosing Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, Tyrese Maxey, and VJ Edgecombe over the Cavaliers and Heat. The move instantly reshapes Philadelphia from play-in hopeful to title contender and closes the book on one of the most-watched free agency sagas in league history.

Elsewhere, New Orleans signed **Trendon Watford** (1yr/$2.85M) and Phoenix added **Haywood Highsmith** (1yr/$3.07M), while Brooklyn brought in forward **Nick Pringle** on an Exhibit 10 deal — the tail end of a very active offseason that already reshuffled Giannis (Miami), Ja Morant (Portland), Jaylen Brown (Philadelphia), and Kawhi Leonard (Toronto, trade still under NBA review).

## 🚴 Cycling

### Vuelta a España Starts Saturday — Pogačar Chasing the Triple Crown
The 81st **Vuelta a España** gets underway Saturday, August 22 in Monaco and runs through September 13 in Granada — 21 stages, roughly 3,275 km, and seven summit finishes across France, Andorra, and Spain, with extreme southern-Spain heat expected to be a factor. **Tadej Pogačar**, fresh off a record-equaling fifth Tour de France title, returns to the Vuelta for the first time since his 2019 debut and is favored to become just the ninth rider ever to complete the Giro–Tour–Vuelta triple crown. He'll face **Wout van Aert**, **Mads Pedersen**, **João Almeida**, and **Oscar Onley** across 23 teams and 184 riders; Giulio Ciccone is a notable absentee, focusing instead on an autumn campaign.
