### OpenAI Disrupts Russian Covert Influence Campaign Run Through ChatGPT
OpenAI banned a cluster of Russia-linked ChatGPT accounts used to run a covert influence operation pushing pro-Kremlin narratives across X, Telegram, Substack, Facebook, and LinkedIn. The operators built the campaign around a fake think tank, the "International Burke Institute," whose website recycled and misattributed real academic work, and used VPNs to get around OpenAI's Russia access block while prompting in Russian and asking the model to scrub linguistic clues of their origin. OpenAI said the operation's real-world reach was limited — most posts got few views, and its associated Telegram channels had only 10,000–20,000 followers each.
