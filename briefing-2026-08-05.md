## 🇨🇿 Czech News

### President Pavel Vetoes Budget Law, Coalition Vows to Override
**Petr Pavel** vetoed an amendment to the budget-responsibility laws that would have let the government overshoot approved spending by up to 10% during a "worsened security situation." Pavel argues it threatens long-term fiscal sustainability and weakens parliament's oversight role. PM **Andrej Babiš** called the veto "irresponsible," and the ruling coalition — which holds 108 of 200 seats, comfortably above the 101 needed — plans to override it when the Chamber of Deputies takes it up on **August 25**.

### Heatwave Tally: 41.5°C in Plzeň Was the National Peak
As the record-breaking heat from earlier this week is tallied, **Plzeň-Bolevec's 41.5°C** stands as the highest reading anywhere in the country, topping its own all-time record of 40.7°C set in June. All 171 Czech weather stations logged new records for the date, and roughly three-quarters of long-running stations (30+ years of data) broke or matched their absolute all-time highs. The heat continues to strain water supplies in the Brno region and fuel wildfire risk in Šumava.
