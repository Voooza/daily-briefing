### Minimum Wage Set for Record Jump to CZK 24,900 in 2027
The government's wage formula points to a minimum monthly wage of **CZK 24,900** from January 2027 — up **CZK 2,500 (11.2%)** from this year's CZK 22,400, the largest increase, in both absolute and percentage terms, on record. The figure comes from applying a 0.446 coefficient to the projected average wage of CZK 55,795 and would push the minimum wage to **44.5%** of the average — the highest ratio Czechia has ever had; final confirmation is expected by the end of September.

### Building Act Override Pushed to September
After the opposition-controlled Senate rejected the government's building-permit overhaul on August 20 — critics call it a developer-friendly "paskvil" — the governing ANO–SPD–Motoristé coalition is preparing to override the veto when the Chamber of Deputies reconvenes in September. Senators from Starostové, ODS, and TOP 09 say they'll challenge the law at the Constitutional Court if the override succeeds.
